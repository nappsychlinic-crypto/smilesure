PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Dental Clinic Website – New Practice, Zero Digital Presence
1. OBJECTIVE & SUCCESS METRICS
Primary Objective

Create a professional, trustworthy, conversion-oriented website for a newly opened dental clinic that:

Establishes medical credibility

Educates first-time patients

Converts visitors into appointment requests

Builds long-term local authority

Success Metrics (KPIs)

Appointment inquiries (calls + WhatsApp + form submissions)

Google Maps direction clicks

Average time on Services & About pages

Local search visibility (Google Business Profile impressions)

Bounce rate below 50% on homepage

2. TARGET USER PERSONAS
Persona 1: First-Time Dental Patient

Age: 22–40

Anxiety around pain and hygiene

Searches: “dentist near me”, “painless root canal”

Needs reassurance and clarity

Persona 2: Family Decision Maker

Age: 30–55

Concerned about kids and elders

Wants safe, long-term care

Needs trust signals and infrastructure proof

Persona 3: Emergency Patient

High urgency

Low patience

Needs fast contact, location, timings
